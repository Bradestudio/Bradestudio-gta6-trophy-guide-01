One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot — has a single moment in Chapter 1 that never comes back: the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen at Jason’s house, follow her to the balcony and accept the meditation. No mission replay exists, so do it then and keep a manual save from Chapter 1. It is not yet confirmed whether that balcony moment is strictly required for the trophy, so this atlas tells you to do it anyway rather than risk it. Every other trophy is still earnable after the story.

Road to the platinum — four stages

About 200 hours in total: roughly 60–80 for the story, the rest for cleanup. Play in order; the tracker counts what you have earned in each stage.

  1. Stage 1 — Main story

    Play Chapters 1 to 5 in order. All twelve story trophies pop as you go, including the gold for the final mission. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation the morning after Down to Earth.

  3. Stage 3 — Activities & collections
